How I'd approach my first 90 days

A starting plan for leading the team. I'd adjust it once I see how the writers work today.

Days 1 to 30

Learn how the team works now. Read the existing content and analytics to see what students search for and what performs. Talk to each writer about what helps them and what slows them down.

Days 31 to 60

Write a short editorial standard covering tone, sourcing from official pages and formatting. Add a review checklist and a shared calendar with owners and deadlines, building on the research template I already use.

Days 61 to 90

Start a monthly report to the co-founder on what performed, what's next and what the team needs. Plan upcoming scholarship cycles with the social media team so content is ready when students start searching.
